A little about me

I’m interested in the part of advertising where creative ideas meet the data behind them. I like looking at how brands show up across digital channels, questioning numbers that don’t quite add up, and finding the story hidden inside a messy set of data. I’m comfortable moving between creative thinking, research, campaign details and the practical work of getting an answer right.

My experience in ad operations and campaign management has taught me to pay attention to the details that can easily get missed — from creative and brand identification to campaign data, quality checks and reporting. I enjoy investigating discrepancies, working with different teams and turning complicated information into something another person can actually understand.

In the end, I am just a copywriter, content strategist, and campaign management analyst drawn to quirky, innovative copy that resonates with brands. Tight deadlines and never-ending client expectations are blessings in disguise for me. And most importantly, I write every day.
