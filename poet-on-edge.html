Poem 01

Party Ghost

How would it feel like to be the ghost of somebody’s story You know your presence, and they.. don’t!! How would it feel like to stand in the parallel space, And making the back of their neck hair stand up What is it with ‘getting to know’ anyway.. Their stories and travels, their plans here onwards To reside the space in parting water by the fingertip The air that they breathe, the silence that creeps in Lurking in the corner of the party they seem to enjoy All the while holding the gaze to invite over Where body is just numb and has fallen still, unmoving Mind races to break through the prison Where I come in light, the demon so well anaesthetised Unseen, unheard, sense paralysed Always there, everywhere; the ghost of the story The party host of the death
Poetry for life
