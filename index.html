A little about me

I’m Parag — a copywriter, content strategist, and campaign management analyst drawn to quirky, innovative copy that resonates with brands. Tight deadlines and never-ending client expectations are blessings in disguise for me. And most importantly, I write every day.

Alongside the creative side, I have over four years of experience in ad operations and campaign management. My work has involved tracking digital media, trafficking creative assets, checking ad quality and analysing campaign data. I enjoy keeping workflows on track, getting to the bottom of data mismatches, making sure campaigns meet compliance standards and turning results into clear performance reports. I also work with digital advertising metrics and SQL-based data checks across the Indian and wider APAC markets.
