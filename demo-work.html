DEMO WORK

Energy Drink — Digital Performance Analysis

A simulated ecommerce analytics project exploring how an energy drink brand could understand its traffic, identify friction in the purchase journey, and turn performance data into practical optimisation ideas.

GA4 Concepts Looker Studio Excel Conversion Optimisation

Self-directed demo project — all figures are simulated

01 / THE BUSINESS QUESTION

Traffic is only the start.

The fictional brand attracts visitors from search, paid media, social platforms, email, and direct traffic. But which visitors show purchase intent, and at what point does interest start to fade?

The objective

Build a clear view of acquisition and ecommerce behaviour, then identify areas worth testing across product discovery, campaign landing pages, and checkout.

The approach

Organise the sample data into channel performance, a purchase funnel, and page-level observations that could be monitored in a Looker Studio dashboard.

03 / WHAT TO INVESTIGATE

From patterns to possibilities

Acquisition quality

Compare channel sessions with purchase conversion rate and cost efficiency. High traffic alone may not indicate high-value traffic.

Purchase friction

Review the transition from product view to cart and checkout. Product information, delivery costs, or payment steps may be worth testing.

04 / RECOMMENDED TESTS

Ideas worth putting to work

TEST 01

Make the product clearer

Test more visible flavour descriptions, pack sizes, price-per-pack information, and product benefits on landing pages.

TEST 02

Reduce checkout friction

Check whether delivery fees appear early enough, whether the form can be simplified, and whether payment options are clear.

TEST 03

Compare channel intent

Use purchase conversion rate and simulated ROAS together before suggesting any change to media investment.

TEST 04

Match message to landing page

Test campaign-specific pages against general product pages and compare product engagement and completed orders.

05 / TOOLS & OUTPUT

What this project demonstrates

A structured approach to turning sample marketing data into an analytics story: define the question, map the funnel, compare channels, and propose measurable next steps.
